Common questions

What is graphic recording or live scribing?

Drawing a talk, workshop, or discussion in real time on a large surface — a whiteboard, flipchart paper, foamcore or digitally on an iPad or similar surface — so the group leaves with a visual, shareable record of what was said as it happened.

Where are you located?

Based in Los Angeles, and regularly traveling for events across the United States and internationally. Get in touch to discuss availability.

What formats do you offer?

I love working live on whiteboards, foamcore, or flipchart paper to make things big and lively, but also work digitally on iPad in Procreate for remote sessions or experiences where digital is a better fit. Standalone illustration, storyboards, and video/animation are also available.

How do I book a graphic recorder for my conference or event?

Reach out by email or phone above. If I'm not able to deliver what your event or project needs I'll help connect you to another graphic recorder or illustrator who can.
